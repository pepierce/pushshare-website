Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Request deletion

Email jj@dosusu.com with your online nickname. After checking, we delete your anonymous account, nickname, rank and records, the rooms you opened and your entries in match results, and let you know when it is done.

Data on your headset

Story progress and settings live only on your headset. Remove them with "Reset all data" in the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Records we do not hold

Purchase records are kept by the Meta Horizon Store. Permanent items can be restored with "Restore purchases" after your account is deleted.
