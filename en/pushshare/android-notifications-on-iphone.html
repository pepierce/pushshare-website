How to get Android notifications on iPhone

When you use a work Android phone or a secondary phone, important alerts often stay on the device you are not holding. PushShare helps by forwarding selected Android notifications to your iPhone.

View the PushShare product page How to get Android notifications on Apple Watch

Summary

  • Forward Android notifications to iPhone.
  • Choose specific apps and keywords instead of forwarding everything.
  • Review recent forwarded messages in the in-app message list.

Why this helps

Many people use more than one phone: a work phone, a secondary phone, or a family device. But most of the time you only carry one main device. PushShare reduces the gap between the phone receiving the alert and the phone you are actually watching.

What you need

You need the Android device that sends notifications and the iPhone that receives them. Both devices should use the same PushShare account. The Android device must allow notification access.

Setup steps

  1. Install PushShare on the Android phone.
  2. Allow notification access.
  3. Install PushShare on iPhone and sign in with the same account.
  4. Select the Android apps you want to forward.
  5. Confirm that the test notification arrives on iPhone.

Select only important notifications

Forwarding every notification can create noise. PushShare lets you select apps and use keyword filters so only useful alerts move to your iPhone.

Message list and deletion

Recent forwarded push messages can be reviewed in the app. You can delete messages, and related server-stored messages are removed when you log out, delete a device, or delete your account.

Quick start

  • Install PushShare on Android and iPhone
  • Connect both devices with the same account
  • Allow notification access on Android
  • Choose apps and keywords
  • Check a test alert on iPhone
