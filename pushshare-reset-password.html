Account help

Reset your password

Enter the email you use for PushShare. We’ll request a new reset email when you press the button.

Get a new reset email

Use the address you signed up with.

Your email is sent to Firebase Authentication to request this email. This page does not save it or use analytics.

What happens next?

  1. Check your inbox and spam or junk folder. Delivery can take a few minutes.
  2. Open the latest reset email promptly. Its link opens the official Firebase page, where you choose your own new password.
  3. Return to PushShare and sign in with your new password.

An old email link expired?

You can keep using this webpage to request a new email. The reset link inside each email expires; this webpage is the reusable starting point. If no email arrives after a few minutes, check the address and spam folder, then try again when the button is available.

Signed in with Google or Apple?

Use the same Google or Apple sign-in button in PushShare. Accounts that only use those methods do not need a PushShare password reset.
