Support scope

Designed and tested for Apple Books on iPhone and iPad

VoiceLeaf prioritizes EPUB audio playback in Apple Books. Audio playback on Android, Google Play Books, and other EPUB readers is not guaranteed.

FAQ

Before you create an EPUB

What can I upload?

Paste text directly or upload a plain TXT file containing material you own or have permission to convert.

Does the EPUB include audio?

You can add normal-speed audio, slow audio, or both. An optional combined MP3 can also be placed at the top of the EPUB.

How long is the download available?

The secure download link is valid for 24 hours. Generated files under the download area are automatically deleted after two days.

Can I use the EPUB on Android or Kindle?

The primary supported reader is Apple Books on iPhone and iPad. Text may open elsewhere, but embedded-audio playback is not guaranteed.
